# Customer-Behaviour-Analysis
This project analyses customer shopping behaviour using Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI to uncover valuable business insights from customer purchase data. The dataset was cleaned, analysed, and visualised to understand customer demographics, purchasing patterns, product performance, discounts, subscriptions, and revenue trends.

## Key Analysis Performed:
Compared total revenue generated by male and female customers.
Identified customers who used discounts but still spent above the average purchase amount.
Found the top 5 highest-rated products based on customer reviews.
Compared average purchase amounts for Standard and Express shipping.
Analysed spending patterns of subscribers and non-subscribers.
Identified products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases.
Segmented customers into New, Returning, and Loyal groups based on purchase history.
Found the top 3 most purchased products in each category.
Analysed whether repeat customers are more likely to subscribe.
Compared revenue contribution across different age groups.
Created interactive dashboards to present key insights and trends.
